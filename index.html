Before you visit

How much will it cost?

Price depends on your destination, package size and weight. Request a DHL quote for staff to review. The online request does not purchase a shipping label.

Do I need a box or a printer?

Ask your counter for packing and label help. Staff confirms available materials and any packing charges.

Can I ship today?

Check your counter’s hours and last DHL collection before traveling. Sending an online request does not book a pickup.
