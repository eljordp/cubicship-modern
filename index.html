About Us

Global logistics,
local service.

Cubic Home Solutions / Cubic Ship brings global logistics and business services to local communities with integrity, care, and trust.

We support customers, small businesses, and independent shipping locations by making professional logistics easier to access at the neighborhood level. Through our DHL Express Service Point service model, Cubic Ship locations are built to be full-service counter hubs.

They are full-service community logistics and business-service hubs for shipping, packing, printing, document support, storage, air freight, ocean freight, ground freight, and everyday business needs.

Every shipment, customer interaction, and branch responsibility matters. That is why we focus on accurate processing, clear documentation, timely follow-up, professional service, and consistent execution across every location we support.

Cubic Ship exists to bridge global logistics capability with local community service, helping people move packages, documents, freight, and business forward with confidence.
