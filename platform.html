Core Flow

One workflow for every business service.

This keeps DHL labels, printing, mailbox, virtual notary, and extra business services inside the same operational pattern.

01

Account

Business customer creates a profile with company, contact, service volume, and security settings.

02

Service

Customer selects DHL label, printing, mailbox, virtual notary, or another business service.

03

Order Code

System generates a secured order number and QR/code for tracking, staff lookup, and counter handoff.

04

Location

Order routes to the designated CubicShip/DHL location based on customer selection or assignment.

05

Staff Work

Staff reviews details, creates the DHL label in the carrier system, uploads the label PDF, and updates status.

06

Customer View

Customer logs in to view status, download/print labels, see notes, and receive notifications.

07

Checkout

Payments can be collected through checkout once pricing and service rules are connected.

08

Audit Log

Every key action is recorded for security: customer changes, staff updates, label uploads, payments, and notifications.

Operations Roadmap

Build the workflow around CRA and file upload.

This avoids promising automated DHL label creation when staff already uses CRA as the label-printing system.

Phase 1: MVPBusiness profiles, service orders, secure codes, location routing, staff updates, label PDF upload, email notifications.
Phase 2: Commerce + SecurityCheckout, receipt flow, SMS/OTP, stronger roles, audit log, label download permissions.
Phase 3: CRA Support + ReportingBetter CRA handoff fields, uploaded label/receipt history, tracking/reference logs, payment reporting, and dashboard exports.
