Agent Onboarding

Interview grading, virtual training, and operating readiness.

Built from the Tamir/Mo operating structure: hire with a scorecard, onboard with a checklist, train virtually, then manage by KPI instead of daily micromanagement.

1. Clear roles and reporting lines

Every agent is assigned a role, location, manager, and escalation path before first service day.

2. Interview scorecard

Grade service mindset, reliability, communication, systems comfort, cash/security judgment, and upsell awareness.

3. Service agreement acknowledgment

Track whether the associate reviewed the service agreement, handbook, standards, and reporting expectations.

4. First-week checklist

Day 1 setup, Day 2 observation, Day 3 supported practice, Day 4 routine work, Day 5 review.

5. Virtual training modules
  • Brand, community, and service mindset
  • Daily operations SOP
  • DHL shipment and drop-off processing
  • OPL, DSS, and reporting discipline
  • Customer experience and complaint handling
  • Escalation, data privacy, safety, and revenue awareness
6. KPI-based management

Track OPL completion, daily summary, shipment/order accuracy, complaint resolution, response time, and training completion.

7. Store communication standard

Move toward store-level communication channels instead of relying on personal devices for operations.

8. DHL support usage

Document DHL representative contact, training needs, system issues, and operational questions.

9. Service quality review

Use coaching, service quality notice, performance improvement, contract review, or service termination language when needed.

10. Pilot before scaling

Test the onboarding and reporting model in selected locations, measure results, refine, then roll out.

Agent scorecards are stored in the secure portal database. Managers can approve, hold, or mark coaching required.

Quote Pipeline

How every quote should move.

These stages become real statuses in the full portal. For now, they give staff a consistent process when answering from Cognito and email.

1. NewCustomer submitted quote request. Check pickup, destination, item count, weight, photos, and date.
2. Needs InfoMissing dimensions, weight, photos, address, timeline, customs details, or service mode.
3. PricingChoose DHL Express, air, ocean, ground, or container path. Confirm cost and margin.
4. SentSend quote response with price, ETA, required documents, and next step.
5. Follow UpFollow up within 24 hours. Mark won, lost, pending, or needs manager review.
