Print, Pack & Paperwork

Labels, customs forms, copies, scans, faxes, passport photos, mailers, boxes, tape, and shipment prep.

  1. Bring the item, document, or label you need handled.

Passport Services

Passport photos, renewal support, application printing, copying, and document prep before mailing.

  1. Bring a valid ID and any passport/application documents you already have.
  2. Take compliant passport photos at the counter.
  3. Print, copy, or organize the required forms.
  4. Ship the packet with tracking when it is ready.

IL DMV Services

Vehicle registration, plates, title transfer support, and related Illinois vehicle paperwork.

  1. Bring your title, ID, insurance, and any vehicle documents.
  2. The counter checks what service is available for your case.
  3. Complete forms and payment at the counter.
  4. Leave with next steps, receipt, or available plates/registration support.

Payment Center

Utilities, rent, mortgage, credit cards, money orders, and other supported bill payment services.

  1. Bring the bill, account number, or payment slip.
  2. Confirm the company, amount, and due date.
  3. Pay at the counter using an accepted method.
  4. Keep the receipt for your records.

Stasher Luggage Storage

Short-term luggage storage for travelers who need to drop bags before pickup, flights, errands, or appointments.

  1. Book online if you already have a Stasher reservation.
  2. Bring your bags, ID, and reservation confirmation.
  3. The counter tags and stores your luggage.
  4. Return during business hours with your claim info.

Virtual Notary Support

Remote online notary guidance plus document prep, scanning, upload support, and identity-ready paperwork.

  1. Call ahead to confirm the document type can be handled remotely.
  2. Bring a valid government ID and unsigned documents.
  3. Prepare scans, uploads, or printed copies as needed.
  4. Complete the online notary session and save the final copy.

Containers & Air Cargo

Air freight, container shipping, oversized items, commercial cargo, and international freight quote support.

  1. Bring dimensions, weight, photos, pickup location, and destination.
  2. Tell us if it is urgent, fragile, commercial, or personal cargo.
  3. We review air, ocean, ground, or container options.
  4. You receive quote next steps and required shipping documents.
