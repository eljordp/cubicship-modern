Before you ship

Do I need a box or a printer?

You can ask the counter for packing and label help. Tell us if the item is unpacked; staff will confirm materials and any packing charges.

Can I send this item internationally?

Describe the contents accurately, including any batteries, liquids or food. Acceptance depends on the item and destination. The counter must confirm eligibility before shipping.

What should I bring?

Bring your items, your request code, the receiver's full address and contact details, and details of the contents and value. Ask the counter which identification and customs documents your shipment needs.

Does the quote include duties and protection?

Ask staff to confirm transport, packing, optional shipment protection, and any duties or taxes separately, including who pays destination charges. Protection and claims depend on the service and applicable terms.

Can I track my request code?

Your CubicShip code identifies this request. Once staff creates the shipment, use the carrier tracking number on the tracking page. Contact your counter about a request that has not shipped.
